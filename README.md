# LP Ledger

Turn your **tastytrade** screenshots into a clean options **track record** (equity curve, win rate, profit factor, per-strategy breakdown) and a **live book & risk** view — entirely in your browser. No backend, no broker login, no upload. Your screenshots never leave your device.

A free build for [Ryan LePiane — LP Options Academy](https://ryanlepiane.substack.com), by [Momentum Phinance](https://momentumphinance.substack.com).

> Not affiliated with tastytrade.
